# (Giant) Chocolate Chip Walnut Cookies

## Equipment

- KitchenAid stand mixer with flat beater
- #6 cookie scoop (about 6 oz per scoop)
- Kitchen scale
- Spatula
- Half sheet pan
- Parchment paper
- Instant-read thermometer

## Ingredients

Note: For chocolate chips, **make sure it's actually cacao and NOT palm oil**. I used `Good & Gather Signature Semi Sweet Chocolate Chips` in the black bag with `45% Cocoa` printed on it from Target (most chocolate chip bags from Good & Gather will NOT work right). I also used `Ghiradelli Premium Baking 72% Cacao Dark Chocolate Chips`. All of the common Toll House bags that I found in local stores were not suitable. If you deviate from this, the rest of the recipe will need to be rebalanced to work right. Take all the shortcuts you want, except for when it comes to the chocolate.

| Ingredient | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Unsalted butter, softened | 113 g |
| Light brown sugar (important, measure correctly, or the cookies will spread too much) | 113 g |
| White sugar (important, measure correctly, or the cookies will spread too much) | 100 g |
| Vanilla extract | 15 g |
| Salt | 6 g |
| Baking powder | 7 g |
| Baking soda | 5 g |
| Freshly grated nutmeg, optional | A chef's pinch |
| Eggs, cold | 100 g (about 2 large) |
| All-purpose flour | 283 g |
| 72% (important, see note above) chocolate chips | 250 g |
| 45% (important, see note above) chocolate chips | 175 g |
| Raw walnut pieces (pecans would be fine too) | 240 g |

## Instructions

1. **Toast the walnuts.** Heat the oven to **160°C (320°F)**. Spread the walnuts on the half sheet and toast for **12-14 minutes**, until fragrant and lightly golden. Let cool completely.
2. **Cream.** Basically just follow Christina Tosi's method. Put the butter, both sugars, vanilla, salt, baking powder, baking soda, and nutmeg in the mixer bowl. Mix on speed 2 to combine, then beat on speed 4 until pale and fluffy, about **8 minutes**. Scrape the bowl halfway through. Keep going until stiff peaks.
4. **Add the eggs.** Add the eggs one at a time, mixing well after each.
5. **Add the flour and mix-ins.** On low speed, mix in the flour just until combined. Mix in the chocolate chips and walnuts until evenly distributed.
6. **Scoop.** Fill the **#6 scoop** and scrape it level with a spatula for each portion. Gently round each portion into a ball without flattening if refrigerating OR flatten to a hockey puck like shape if freezing.
7. **Chill.** Cover and refrigerate for **at least 12 hours** OR freeze (be sure to flatten before freezing).
8. **Prepare to bake.** Heat the oven to **180°C (350°F), conventional**, with a rack at the top. Line the half sheet with parchment paper. Place up to **4 chilled or frozen dough balls/pucks** on it, spaced well apart.
9. **Bake.** Bake for **24–26 minutes**, checking from **20 minutes**, until puffed and lightly browned. Aim for **82°C (180°F)** in the center, avoiding chocolate pockets with the thermometer.
10. **Cool.** Leave the cookies on the half sheet until warm and firm enough to lift. Let the pan cool before baking another batch.
